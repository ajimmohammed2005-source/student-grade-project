How to play & what you will learn
  • Goal: choose a model that predicts new data well. Each level has hidden data (the test set) that is revealed only when you press the lock-in button. Your score is how close you get to the best possible.
  • Slider: model complexity = degree of a polynomial curve fitted to the dots (degree 1 = straight line). The curve is fitted for real, in your browser, every time you move the slider.
  • Learning objectives: (1) explain overfitting and underfitting; (2) see why training error alone is misleading and why we hold data back (train / validation / test); (3) see how more data tames a flexible model; (4) see that on real, noisy data a simple model often wins.
  • Tip: play the levels in order. Press New data to replay a level with a different random sample.
