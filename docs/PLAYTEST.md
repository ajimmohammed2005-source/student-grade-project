## Protocol (10 minutes per player)

1. Give the player `partB/index.html` (or the GitHub Pages link) and **say nothing** about overfitting.
2. Ask them to play Levels 1 to 4 while thinking aloud.
3. Before they start, ask: *"Is a model with lower training error always better? Why or why not?"* Write down their answer.
4. After they finish, ask the same question again, and also: *"In your own words, what is overfitting?"*
5. In the game, type their name and press **Create my results summary**. Paste the output below.
6. Ask for feedback: what was confusing, what was fun, what would they change?
